About Us

Beard Papa’s is a Japanese pastry brand famous for its delicious cream puffs. Founded in 1999 in Osaka, Japan, by Yuji Hirota, it started as a small shop offering freshly made puff pastries filled with rich and creamy vanilla custard. The name Beard Papa’s comes from the character of “Papa” (with a beard), referring to the round puff pastry that resembles a head with a beard.

Over the years, Beard Papa’s has expanded rapidly, reaching international markets such as the United States, Canada, Malaysia, and Indonesia. In Indonesia, Beard Papa’s has gained popularity, with its first store opening in Plaza Ambarrukmo, Yogyakarta, marking a significant step in introducing the brand to a larger audience in the country.

Beard Papa’s cream puffs are renowned for their crispy, golden exterior and soft, sweet cream filling. In addition to the classic vanilla flavor, the brand also offers various other cream filling flavors, such as chocolate, matcha, and more, catering to diverse customer preferences.
